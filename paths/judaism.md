# Judaism

The study of Judaism as history, texts, thought and lived practice: the Hebrew Bible, the rabbis and the Talmud, Jewish life in the medieval Islamic and Christian worlds, philosophy and mysticism, modern movements, the Holocaust, Zionism and Jewish life today. About 250 to 300 hours over ten to twelve months.

**Access and balance.** Every title is labelled **Free**, **Free + paid** or **Paid**. Sefaria offers Jewish texts free in Hebrew and English, and many archives and museums are online. This is an academic path, neither promoting nor criticising Judaism. It presents Jewish traditions in their own words and through scholarship, covers Ashkenazi, Sephardi, Mizrahi and other communities, and treats contested modern politics through several perspectives.

# About Judaism

Judaism is the religion, culture and civilisation of the Jewish people, rooted in the Hebrew Bible and the covenant between God and Israel, and developed over three thousand years through rabbinic interpretation, law, practice and community.

This path begins with accessible introductions, studies the Hebrew Bible, follows the rabbis and the Talmud, explores Jewish life and thought in the medieval world, examines modern movements and the Holocaust, and ends with Zionism, Israel and Jewish life worldwide today.

**Trivia**
- There are about 15–16 million Jews worldwide, most in Israel and the United States.
- The Dead Sea Scrolls, found from 1947 onwards, include the oldest known manuscripts of the Hebrew Bible, from about the 3rd century BCE.
- The Babylonian Talmud runs to about 2.5 million words in its standard edition.
- Maimonides, the great medieval philosopher, also served as a court physician in Cairo.

### Timeline
- **c. 1000 BCE · The kingdom of Israel** under David and Solomon, in biblical tradition.
- **586 BCE · The First Temple** is destroyed; exile in Babylon.
- **70 CE · The Second Temple** is destroyed by Rome.
- **c. 200 CE · The Mishnah** is compiled.
- **c. 500 CE · The Babylonian Talmud** takes shape.
- **1190 · Maimonides's *Guide for the Perplexed*** is completed.
- **1492 · Spain** expels its Jews.
- **1780s · The Haskalah (Jewish Enlightenment)** begins.
- **1939–45 · The Holocaust** murders six million Jews.
- **1948 · The State of Israel** is founded.

# What you will be able to do

At the end of this path you will know Judaism the way a strong first year of a university Jewish studies course expects. You will be able to:

- **Read key texts:** the Hebrew Bible, Mishnah, Talmud and later works.
- **Explain practice:** Sabbath, festivals, food laws, prayer and life-cycle rituals.
- **Trace Jewish history:** across the Middle East, Europe, North Africa and beyond.
- **Explain thought and movements:** philosophy, mysticism, Hasidism, Reform, Orthodox and more.
- **Discuss modern questions:** the Holocaust, antisemitism, Zionism, Israel and diaspora life.
- **For the Mastery certificate:** have written the Scholar challenges and passed the final mastery check.

These goals are a draft standard. Scholars of Judaism and members of several Jewish communities will review the path before it is published.
