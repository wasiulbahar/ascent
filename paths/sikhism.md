# Sikhism

The study of Sikhism as history, scripture, thought and lived practice: Guru Nanak and the ten Gurus, the Guru Granth Sahib and its music, the Khalsa, seva and langar, Sikh empire and colonial rule, Partition and 1984, and the global Sikh diaspora. About 250 to 300 hours over ten to twelve months.

**Access and balance.** Every title is labelled **Free**, **Free + paid** or **Paid**. The Guru Granth Sahib is free online with translations, and many histories and archives are free. This is an academic path, neither promoting nor criticising Sikhism. It presents Sikh traditions in their own words alongside scholarship, and treats contested modern history through several perspectives.

# About Sikhism

Sikhism began in the Punjab region of South Asia with Guru Nanak (1469–1539). With around 25–30 million followers, it is one of the world's major religions, centred on devotion to one God, honest work, sharing, equality and service.

This path begins with accessible introductions, studies Guru Nanak and the Gurus, reads the Guru Granth Sahib and its music, explores the Khalsa and Sikh practice, follows Sikh history through empire, Partition and 1984, and ends with the global diaspora and Sikh identity today.

**Trivia**
- The Golden Temple (Harmandir Sahib) in Amritsar serves free meals to tens of thousands of people every day in its langar.
- The Guru Granth Sahib includes hymns by Hindu and Muslim saints as well as the Sikh Gurus.
- Sikhs make up about 2% of India's population but have a large diaspora in Britain, Canada and beyond.
- Ranjit Singh ruled a powerful Sikh Empire in the Punjab from 1799 to 1839.

### Timeline
- **1469 · Guru Nanak** is born.
- **1604 · The Adi Granth** is compiled by Guru Arjan.
- **1606 · Guru Arjan** is executed under Mughal rule.
- **1675 · Guru Tegh Bahadur** is executed in Delhi.
- **1699 · Guru Gobind Singh** founds the Khalsa.
- **1708 · The Guru Granth Sahib** becomes the eternal Guru.
- **1799–1839 · Maharaja Ranjit Singh** rules the Sikh Empire.
- **1849 · Britain** annexes the Punjab.
- **1947 · Partition** divides the Punjab.
- **1984 · Operation Blue Star** and anti-Sikh violence follow.

# What you will be able to do

At the end of this path you will know Sikhism the way a strong first year of a university religious studies course expects. You will be able to:

- **Explain core teachings:** one God, naam, seva, equality and the Gurus' message.
- **Describe the Guru Granth Sahib:** its authors, structure, music and role.
- **Explain practice:** the Khalsa, the five Ks, gurdwaras and langar.
- **Trace Sikh history:** from the Gurus to empire, Partition, 1984 and diaspora.
- **Discuss Sikh identity today:** turbans, discrimination, politics and community life.
- **For the Mastery certificate:** have written the Scholar challenges and passed the final mastery check.

These goals are a draft standard. Scholars of Sikhism and Sikh community members from several countries will review the path before it is published.
