# Buddhism

The study of Buddhism as history, philosophy and lived practice: the Buddha and early teachings, the spread across Asia, Theravāda, Mahāyāna and Vajrayāna traditions, Buddhist philosophy, art and society, and Buddhism in the modern world. About 250 to 300 hours over ten to twelve months.

**Access and balance.** Every title is labelled **Free**, **Free + paid** or **Paid**. Many Buddhist texts are free in translation, and museums share Buddhist art online. This is an academic path, neither promoting nor criticising Buddhism: it presents traditions in their own words alongside scholarly perspectives, and covers difficult history as well as ideals.

# About Buddhism

Buddhism began with Siddhārtha Gautama, the Buddha, in northern India around the 5th century BCE, and spread across Asia and later the world. With around 500 million followers, it includes many traditions, practices and philosophies.

This path begins with accessible introductions, studies the Buddha and early teachings, follows Buddhism's spread across Asia, explores the major traditions, examines Buddhist philosophy, and ends with art, society, politics and Buddhism in the modern world.

**Trivia**
- There are around 500 million Buddhists, most in East and Southeast Asia.
- The Diamond Sūtra, printed in China in 868 CE, is the oldest dated printed book.
- Borobudur in Indonesia, built in the 8th–9th centuries, is the world's largest Buddhist temple.
- Buddhism almost disappeared from India by the 13th century and was later revived, including by B. R. Ambedkar's mass conversion in 1956.

### Timeline
- **c. 5th century BCE · The Buddha** teaches in northern India.
- **c. 250 BCE · Emperor Ashoka** supports Buddhism and sends missions.
- **c. 1st century BCE · The Pāli Canon** is written down in Sri Lanka.
- **1st–2nd century CE · Mahāyāna sūtras** spread; Buddhism reaches China.
- **c. 150–250 CE · Nāgārjuna** founds the Madhyamaka school.
- **6th century · Buddhism** reaches Japan from Korea.
- **7th–8th century · Buddhism** spreads in Tibet.
- **868 · The Diamond Sūtra** is printed in China.
- **1956 · B. R. Ambedkar** leads a mass conversion in India.
- **1959 · The 14th Dalai Lama** goes into exile in India.

# What you will be able to do

At the end of this path you will know Buddhism the way a strong first year of a university religious studies course expects. You will be able to:

- **Explain core teachings:** the Four Noble Truths, the Eightfold Path, karma, rebirth and not-self.
- **Trace Buddhism's history:** from India across Asia and the world.
- **Compare traditions:** Theravāda, Mahāyāna, Vajrayāna, Zen and Pure Land.
- **Engage with Buddhist philosophy:** emptiness, mind and ethics.
- **Discuss Buddhism in society:** art, monasticism, politics and modern adaptations.
- **For the Mastery certificate:** have written the Scholar challenges and passed the final mastery check.

These goals are a draft standard. Scholars of Buddhism and Buddhist practitioners from several traditions and countries will review the path before it is published.

**How stages work.** Each work ends with a short quiz. Score 70% or more across a stage's quizzes to pass it. If you fall short, you can retake the quizzes, or continue anyway: the stage then stays marked **Not finished** until you come back and pass it.
