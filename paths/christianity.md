# Christianity

The study of Christianity as history, texts, thought and lived practice: Jesus and the New Testament, the early church and its councils, Orthodox, Catholic and Protestant traditions, theology and mysticism, Christianity in Africa, Asia and Latin America, and Christianity in the modern world. About 250 to 300 hours over ten to twelve months.

**Access and balance.** Every title is labelled **Free**, **Free + paid** or **Paid**. Bible translations, early Christian writings and many classic theological works are free. This is an academic path, neither promoting nor criticising Christianity. It presents traditions in their own words alongside scholarship, gives full weight to Christianity in the Global South, and covers difficult history honestly.

# About Christianity

Christianity is the faith centred on Jesus of Nazareth, believed by Christians to be the Son of God. With about 2.4 billion followers, it is the world's largest religion, with Catholic, Orthodox, Protestant, Pentecostal and many other traditions.

This path begins with accessible introductions, studies Jesus and the New Testament, follows the early church, examines the great divisions and traditions, explores theology and mysticism, and ends with global Christianity and the modern world.

**Trivia**
- There are about 2.4 billion Christians, and today most live in Africa, Latin America and Asia rather than Europe and North America.
- The Ethiopian Orthodox Church is one of the oldest Christian churches, with roots in the 4th century.
- The Gutenberg Bible, printed around 1455, was the first major book printed with movable type in Europe.
- Pentecostalism, which began in the early 20th century, now has hundreds of millions of followers worldwide.

### Timeline
- **c. 30 CE · Jesus** is crucified in Jerusalem.
- **c. 50–60 CE · Paul's letters** are written.
- **c. 70–100 CE · The Gospels** are written.
- **313 · The Edict of Milan** tolerates Christianity in the Roman Empire.
- **325 · The Council of Nicaea** defines key beliefs.
- **c. 330s · Ethiopia's King Ezana** adopts Christianity.
- **1054 · The East–West Schism** divides Orthodox and Catholic churches.
- **1517 · Martin Luther** starts the Protestant Reformation.
- **1906 · The Azusa Street Revival** spreads Pentecostalism.
- **1962–65 · The Second Vatican Council** reforms the Catholic Church.

# What you will be able to do

At the end of this path you will know Christianity the way a strong first year of a university religious studies course expects. You will be able to:

- **Read key texts:** the Gospels, Paul's letters and early Christian writings.
- **Trace Christian history:** from the early church to today's global Christianity.
- **Compare traditions:** Orthodox, Catholic, Protestant, Pentecostal and others.
- **Explain theology:** Trinity, incarnation, salvation, sacraments and ethics.
- **Discuss Christianity in society:** mission, colonialism, social justice and secularisation.
- **For the Mastery certificate:** have written the Scholar challenges and passed the final mastery check.

These goals are a draft standard. Scholars of Christianity and members of several Christian traditions from different regions will review the path before it is published.
