# Animation

How drawings, puppets and pixels are brought to life: the principles of animation, timing and storyboards, world traditions from silhouette films to anime, 3D and computer animation, story, acting and sound, and making and sharing your own short films. About 150 to 200 hours over six to nine months, most of it animating.

**Access and balance.** Every title is labelled **Free**, **Free + paid** or **Paid**. Free software (Pencil2D, Blender, Synfig), video lessons and classic films cover the core, and a few books are worth borrowing. A phone or computer is enough to start. The path celebrates animation from Europe, Japan, Russia, the Americas and beyond.

# About animation

Animation creates the illusion of movement by showing a sequence of images. It includes hand-drawn cartoons, stop motion with puppets or clay, cut-out silhouettes, 3D computer animation and motion graphics.

This path begins with the principles and free tools, teaches timing, walk cycles and storyboarding, explores world traditions and stop motion, moves into 3D animation and the computer-animation revolution, studies story, acting and sound, and ends with the industry, motion graphics, accessibility and making your own short film.

**Trivia**
- *The Adventures of Prince Achmed* (1926) by Lotte Reiniger is the oldest surviving animated feature film, made with cut-out silhouettes.
- *Toy Story* (1995) was the first feature film made entirely with computer animation.
- Yuri Norstein's *Hedgehog in the Fog* (1975) has been voted the greatest animated film of all time in an international poll.
- *Big Buck Bunny* (2008) was made entirely with the free, open-source software Blender.

### Timeline
- **1832 · Joseph Plateau** creates the phenakistiscope, an early animation toy.
- **1908 · Émile Cohl** makes *Fantasmagorie*, an early hand-drawn cartoon.
- **1914 · Winsor McCay** presents *Gertie the Dinosaur*.
- **1926 · Lotte Reiniger** releases *The Adventures of Prince Achmed*.
- **1928 · *Steamboat Willie*** popularises synchronised sound in cartoons.
- **1960 · The Annecy International Animation Film Festival** is founded.
- **1975 · Yuri Norstein** makes *Hedgehog in the Fog*.
- **1995 · *Toy Story*** becomes the first fully computer-animated feature.
- **2001 · Hayao Miyazaki** releases *Spirited Away*.
- **2008 · The Blender Foundation** releases *Big Buck Bunny*.

# What you will be able to do

At the end of this path you will animate with the skill of a committed student animator. You will be able to:

- **Apply the principles:** squash and stretch, timing, spacing, anticipation and follow-through.
- **Animate characters:** bouncing balls, walks, acting and lip sync.
- **Plan films:** storyboards, animatics and story structure.
- **Use different techniques:** 2D, stop motion, cut-out and 3D.
- **Make and share work:** complete a short film and understand the industry.
- **For the Mastery certificate:** have written the Scholar challenges and passed the final mastery check.

These goals are a draft standard. Animators and animation teachers from more than one country will confirm them before the path is published.

**How stages work.** Each work ends with a short quiz. Score 70% or more across a stage's quizzes to pass it. If you fall short, you can retake the quizzes, or continue anyway: the stage then stays marked **Not finished** until you come back and pass it.

# Final mastery check

Finishing all six stages makes you a capable animator. To earn the **Ascent Mastery: Animation** certificate, you also complete:

1. **The grand quiz.** 40 game-style questions across all six stages. Pass mark 80%, with unlimited retakes.
2. **Exercise reel.** A reel of a bouncing ball, a walk cycle, a weight lift and an acting shot.
3. **Short film.** A finished animated short of at least one minute, with sound.
4. **Capstone essay.** 2,500 words on one of the Stage 6 questions or on an animation tradition. Strong essays are read by an expert reviewer.
5. **Explain it.** Teach a friend the principles of squash and stretch and timing with a simple animation.

The certificate needs every stage passed.
