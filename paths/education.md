# Education

From how people learn to how teachers teach, how school systems differ around the world, and the arguments about what education is for. About 250 to 300 hours over ten to twelve months.

**Access and balance.** Every title is labelled **Free**, **Free + paid** or **Paid**. Many key sources, such as international reports, classic texts and research summaries, are free. This path teaches knowledge about education; it is not a teaching qualification.

# About education

Education is the study of learning and teaching: how minds acquire knowledge and skill, how teachers can help, how schools and universities are organised, and what societies want education to do. It draws on psychology, philosophy, sociology, economics and history.

This path begins with books that show why learning matters, teaches the science of how people learn, covers teaching and assessment, compares education around the world, reads the classic thinkers, and ends with today's debates about technology, inclusion and the value of schooling.

**Trivia**
- The University of al-Qarawiyyin in Fez, Morocco, founded by Fatima al-Fihri in 859, is often cited as the oldest university still operating.
- Comenius's *Orbis Pictus*, published in 1658, is often called the first picture book made for children.
- In 1885 Hermann Ebbinghaus measured how quickly we forget, by memorising nonsense syllables himself: the “forgetting curve”.
- Article 26 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948) says everyone has the right to education.

### Timeline
- **c. 387 BCE · Plato's Academy** opens in Athens.
- **859 · Al-Qarawiyyin** founded in Fez.
- **1088 · The University of Bologna** Traditionally dated as Europe's first university.
- **1658 · Comenius** *Orbis Pictus*, a picture book for learning.
- **1762 · Rousseau** *Emile, or On Education*.
- **1907 · Montessori** Opens her first Casa dei Bambini in Rome.
- **1916 · Dewey** *Democracy and Education*.
- **1968 · Freire** *Pedagogy of the Oppressed*.
- **2000 · PISA** The first international tests of 15-year-olds.
- **2015 · Sustainable Development Goal 4** Quality education for all, agreed by UN members.

# What you will be able to do

At the end of this path you will know education the way a strong first year of a university education degree expects. You will be able to:

- **Explain how people learn:** memory, practice, feedback and motivation.
- **Use evidence on teaching:** retrieval, spacing, formative assessment and their limits.
- **Compare education systems:** what different countries do and what international data can and can't show.
- **Read the classic thinkers:** Rousseau, Dewey, Montessori, Vygotsky, Freire and Bruner.
- **Weigh the debates:** technology, inclusion, and what schooling is worth.
- **For the Mastery certificate:** have written the Scholar challenges and passed the final mastery check.

5. **Explain it.** Answer three unseen questions as if to a curious friend. For example: “Why is testing yourself better than rereading?”

The certificate needs every stage passed. It shows knowledge of education; it is not a teaching qualification.
