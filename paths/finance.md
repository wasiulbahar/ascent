# Finance

How money, banks and markets work: what money is, how banks create it, how markets price risk, why bubbles and crises happen, how they are regulated, and the frontiers of global finance. About 250 to 300 hours over ten to twelve months.

**Access and balance.** Every title is labelled **Free**, **Free + paid** or **Paid**. Central banks, the IMF and the BIS publish clear free explanations, and several classics are out of copyright. The path presents mainstream theory and its critics. **It is not financial or investment advice**: it explains how finance works, not what anyone should buy.

# About finance

Finance studies how people, companies and governments raise, store, lend, invest and manage money over time and under risk. It covers money and banking, financial markets, the pricing of risk and return, central banks, financial crises and regulation, and finance's role in development.

This path begins with lively histories of money and a famous crisis, teaches the basics of money, banking and investment, studies markets and their manias, reads the key theories and their behavioural critics, examines crises and regulation, and ends with global finance: microcredit, Islamic finance and digital money.

**Trivia**
- The people of Yap in the Pacific used huge stone discs as money; ownership changed even when a stone never moved, showing that money is really a system of credit and trust.
- Most money in modern economies is created by commercial banks when they make loans, as the Bank of England explained in 2014.
- Walter Bagehot's 1873 rule for central banks in a panic: lend freely, at a high rate, against good collateral.
- In the global crisis of 2008, the failure of Lehman Brothers on 15 September triggered panic around the world.

### Timeline
- **c. 600 BCE · The first coins** in Lydia, in today's Turkey.
- **11th century · Paper money** in China.
- **1602 · The Dutch East India Company** issues tradable shares.
- **1637 · Tulip mania** in the Dutch Republic.
- **1694 · The Bank of England** is founded.
- **1873 · Bagehot's *Lombard Street***
- **1929 · The Wall Street Crash** and the Great Depression.
- **1944 · Bretton Woods** creates the IMF and World Bank.
- **1952 · Markowitz** Portfolio theory.
- **2008 · The global financial crisis**
- **2009 · Bitcoin** is launched.

# What you will be able to do

At the end of this path you will know finance the way a strong first year of a university finance degree expects. You will be able to:

- **Explain money and banking:** what money is, how banks create it, and what central banks do.
- **Explain markets:** stocks, bonds, interest rates, risk and return.
- **Use the core theories:** time value of money, diversification and market efficiency, and their critics.
- **Explain crises:** bubbles, panics and the 2008 crash, and how regulation responds.
- **Weigh global debates:** microcredit, Islamic finance, digital money and finance's place in society.
- **For the Mastery certificate:** have written the Scholar challenges and passed the final mastery check.

These goals are a draft standard. Finance scholars and practitioners from more than one country will confirm them before the path is published. Knowledge only: never financial or investment advice.
