# Personal Finance

How to understand and manage money: budgeting and saving, debt and credit, interest and inflation, banking and digital money, insurance, investing basics, pensions, scams, and the psychology of money, explained for people in any country. About 250 to 300 hours over ten to twelve months.

**Access and balance.** Every title is labelled **Free**, **Free + paid** or **Paid**. Free guides from regulators, central banks and nonprofit educators cover the core. Rules, products and taxes differ by country, so the path teaches principles and shows you how to find trustworthy local information. **It is education only: not personal financial, investment or tax advice. It never recommends specific products.**

# About personal finance

Personal finance is how individuals and households earn, spend, save, borrow, protect and invest money. Good financial knowledge helps people avoid harmful debt and scams, cope with shocks, and plan for the future.

This path begins with how money works and the psychology behind our choices, teaches budgeting and saving, explains interest, debt and credit, covers banking, digital money and insurance, introduces investing principles and retirement, and ends with protecting yourself from scams and finding trustworthy advice.

**Trivia**
- Only about one in three adults worldwide are financially literate, according to a 2014 global survey by S&P.
- About 1.4 billion adults worldwide had no bank or mobile money account in 2021, according to the World Bank's Global Findex.
- At 7% a year, money roughly doubles in about 10 years: the “rule of 72” says 72 ÷ 7 ≈ 10.
- Inflation of just 3% a year cuts the buying power of money by about a quarter in 10 years.

### Timeline
- **c. 600 BCE · Coins** appear in Lydia.
- **1100s · Paper money** spreads in Song China.
- **1720 · The South Sea Bubble** ruins many investors.
- **1880s · Pensions** begin in Germany under Bismarck.
- **1929 · The Wall Street Crash** wipes out savings.
- **1950s–60s · Credit cards** spread.
- **1976 · The first index fund** for ordinary investors is launched.
- **2007 · M-Pesa** brings mobile money to Kenya.
- **2008 · The global financial crisis** follows risky lending.
- **2020s · Digital payments and app-based investing** spread rapidly.

# What you will be able to do

At the end of this path you will understand personal finance the way a strong introductory course expects. You will be able to:

- **Budget and save:** track income and spending, and build an emergency fund.
- **Understand interest, inflation and debt:** compound interest, APR and the true cost of borrowing.
- **Use financial services safely:** bank accounts, mobile money, cards and insurance.
- **Explain investing principles:** risk, diversification, fees and time.
- **Protect yourself:** spot scams, read the small print and find trustworthy local advice.
- **For the Mastery certificate:** have written the Scholar challenges and passed the final mastery check.

These goals are a draft standard. Financial educators and consumer protection experts from more than one country will confirm them before the path is published. Education only: not personal financial advice.
