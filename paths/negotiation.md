# Negotiation

How to reach agreements that work: preparing with alternatives and interests, the psychology of bargaining, difficult conversations, culture and multiparty deals, mediation and peace processes, and negotiating ethically in work and life. About 100 to 130 hours over four to six months.

**Access and balance.** Every title is labelled **Free**, **Free + paid** or **Paid**. Free courses, research summaries, games and peace agreement archives cover the core, and a few books are worth borrowing. Practise in real situations, from buying something to agreeing chores. The path draws on negotiations and peace processes from many regions.

# About negotiation

Negotiation is how people with different interests reach agreement, from a salary or a sale to a business deal, a family decision, a trade treaty or a peace accord.

This path starts with lively introductions and a game about trust, teaches preparation, alternatives and interests, explores the psychology of bargaining and difficult conversations, covers culture, multiparty deals and mediation, studies peace processes, and ends with ethics and practice.

**Trivia**
- The Camp David talks between Egypt and Israel in 1978 lasted 13 days and produced a peace treaty the following year.
- Colombia's 2016 peace agreement was narrowly rejected in a referendum, then revised and approved by Congress.
- John Nash proposed a mathematical solution to bargaining problems in 1950.
- The Good Friday Agreement of 1998 was approved by referendums in both Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland.

### Timeline
- **1648 · The Peace of Westphalia** ends the Thirty Years' War after years of negotiation.
- **1815 · The Congress of Vienna** redraws Europe after the Napoleonic Wars.
- **1950 · John Nash** proposes a solution to the bargaining problem.
- **1978 · The Camp David Accords** are negotiated.
- **1981 · Fisher and Ury** publish *Getting to Yes*.
- **1983 · The Program on Negotiation** is founded at Harvard Law School.
- **1998 · The Good Friday Agreement** is signed.
- **2015 · The Paris Agreement** is reached by nearly 200 countries.
- **2016 · Colombia's government and the FARC** sign a peace agreement.

# What you will be able to do

At the end of this path you will negotiate the way a skilled professional does. You will be able to:

- **Prepare well:** interests, alternatives (BATNA), the zone of agreement and options.
- **Bargain wisely:** anchoring, concessions, creating and claiming value.
- **Handle people:** emotions, difficult conversations, power and bias.
- **Work across settings:** cultures, coalitions, mediation and peace processes.
- **Negotiate ethically:** honesty, fairness and lasting agreements.
- **For the Mastery certificate:** have written the Scholar challenges and passed the final mastery check.

These goals are a draft standard. Negotiators, mediators and teachers from more than one country will confirm them before the path is published.

**How stages work.** Each work ends with a short quiz. Score 70% or more across a stage's quizzes to pass it. If you fall short, you can retake the quizzes, or continue anyway: the stage then stays marked **Not finished** until you come back and pass it.
