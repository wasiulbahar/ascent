# Business and management

From how a business makes money to how people are led, how strategy works, and what business owes society. About 250 to 300 hours over ten to twelve months.

**Access and balance.** Every title is labelled **Free**, **Free + paid** or **Paid**. Several core textbooks are free from OpenStax. This path teaches knowledge about business and management: **it is not financial, investment or legal advice**.

# About business and management

Business is how people organise to make goods and services others want, at a cost they can sustain. Management is the work of getting people, money and time to achieve a shared aim. Studying them means learning how firms create value, keep accounts, market, organise work, lead people, choose strategies and answer to society.

This path begins with founders' stories and the basics of any business, teaches accounting, marketing and operations, moves to managing people and organisations, reaches strategy and finance, reads the ideas that shaped management, and ends with the debates about what business is for.

**Trivia**
- Luca Pacioli described double-entry bookkeeping, still the basis of accounting, in a mathematics book printed in Venice in 1494.
- The Dutch East India Company, founded in 1602, is often called the first company to issue shares that the public could trade.
- Henry Ford's moving assembly line, started in 1913, cut the time to build a car from about 12 hours to about 90 minutes.
- Toyota lets any worker on the line pull a cord to stop production when they see a problem.

### Timeline
- **1494 · Pacioli** Double-entry bookkeeping in print.
- **1602 · The Dutch East India Company** Shares traded by the public.
- **1776 · Adam Smith** The division of labour in the pin factory.
- **1911 · Taylor** *The Principles of Scientific Management*.
- **1913 · Ford's moving assembly line**
- **1954 · Drucker** *The Practice of Management*.
- **1980 · Porter** *Competitive Strategy* and the five forces.
- **1997 · Christensen** Disruptive innovation.
- **2000s · Platforms** Firms that connect users, not just sell to them, rise to the top.

# What you will be able to do

At the end of this path you will know business and management the way a strong first year of a university business degree expects. You will be able to:

- **Read the basic accounts:** a balance sheet, an income statement and a cash-flow statement.
- **Explain how firms reach customers:** marketing, segmentation and the four Ps.
- **Explain how work is organised:** operations, teams, motivation and culture.
- **Analyse strategy:** the five forces, competitive advantage and disruption.
- **Weigh the debates:** shareholders or stakeholders, and what business owes society.
- **For the Mastery certificate:** have written the Scholar challenges and passed the final mastery check.

These goals are a draft standard. Business teachers and managers from more than one country will confirm them before the path is published. Knowledge only: never financial or investment advice.
