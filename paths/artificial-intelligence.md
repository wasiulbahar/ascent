# Artificial intelligence

What AI is and how it works: from the ideas of Turing to neural networks, language models and reinforcement learning, what AI does to society, and the debates about its future. About 250 to 300 hours over ten to twelve months.

**Access and balance.** Every title is labelled **Free**, **Free + paid** or **Paid**. Many of the best AI courses and papers are free. The path covers both how AI works and the arguments about its risks and benefits, from several points of view. Later stages need some Python and school maths; the path points to free help.

# About artificial intelligence

Artificial intelligence builds computer systems that do tasks we associate with intelligence: recognising images, understanding and producing language, playing games, making predictions and decisions. Modern AI mostly learns from data, using neural networks trained on vast amounts of examples.

This path begins with clear books on what AI can and can't do, teaches the foundations of machine learning, covers deep learning and language models, reinforcement learning and scientific breakthroughs, weighs AI's effects on society and how it is governed, and ends with the debates about where it is heading.

**Trivia**
- The term “artificial intelligence” was coined for a 1956 summer workshop at Dartmouth College.
- In 2016 DeepMind's AlphaGo beat the Go champion Lee Sedol four games to one, a decade earlier than many experts expected.
- The 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry went partly to Demis Hassabis and John Jumper for AlphaFold, which predicts protein structures.
- The 2017 paper “Attention Is All You Need” introduced the transformer, the design behind most modern language models.

### Timeline
- **1950 · Turing** “Computing Machinery and Intelligence” and the imitation game.
- **1956 · The Dartmouth workshop** names the field.
- **1958 · The perceptron** An early learning machine.
- **1970s and late 1980s · “AI winters”** Funding and hopes collapse.
- **1997 · Deep Blue** beats Kasparov at chess.
- **2012 · AlexNet** Deep learning transforms image recognition.
- **2016 · AlphaGo** beats Lee Sedol.
- **2017 · The transformer** is introduced.
- **2020 · AlphaFold 2** solves much of protein-structure prediction.
- **2022 onwards · Large language models** reach the public.

# What you will be able to do

At the end of this path you will know artificial intelligence the way a strong first year of a university AI course expects. You will be able to:

- **Explain how machines learn:** data, models, training, and evaluation.
- **Explain neural networks:** including deep learning and transformers, in plain terms.
- **Explain other approaches:** search, reinforcement learning and probabilistic reasoning.
- **Judge AI claims:** what systems can and can't do, and how they fail.
- **Weigh the debates:** bias, labour, power, regulation, safety and the long-term future.
- **For the Mastery certificate:** have written the Scholar challenges and passed the final mastery check.

These goals are a draft standard. AI researchers and AI ethicists from more than one country will confirm them before the path is published.
