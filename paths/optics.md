# Optics and Photonics

The science of light: rays, lenses and the eye, waves, interference and polarisation, light and matter from spectra to lasers and LEDs, photonics from fibre optics to holograms and solar cells, and frontiers from cloaking to gravitational-wave detectors. About 150 to 200 hours over six to eight months.

**Access and balance.** Every title is labelled **Free**, **Free + paid** or **Paid**. Free textbooks, simulations and Nobel materials cover the core. You need school physics and trigonometry; some calculus helps from Stage 3. Simple experiments with a torch, a glass of water and a CD are part of the path.

# About optics

Optics is the study of light: how it travels, bends, reflects, interferes and interacts with matter. Photonics applies it in lasers, fibres, cameras, displays, sensors and solar cells.

This path begins with what light is and early optics across cultures, studies rays, lenses and instruments, moves to waves, interference and polarisation, explores light and matter in spectra, lasers and LEDs, covers photonic technologies, and ends with frontiers and research.

**Trivia**
- Around 1021, Ibn al-Haytham explained that we see because light enters the eye, not because the eye sends out rays.
- Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth.
- Charles Kao, the “father of fibre optics”, showed in 1966 that very pure glass could carry light signals over long distances.
- In 2018 Donna Strickland became only the third woman to win the Nobel Prize in Physics, for work on intense laser pulses.

### Timeline
- **c. 1021 · Ibn al-Haytham** completes his *Book of Optics*.
- **1608 · Hans Lipperhey** applies for a patent on the telescope.
- **1621 · Willebrord Snell** finds the law of refraction.
- **1704 · Isaac Newton** publishes *Opticks*.
- **1801 · Thomas Young** shows light interferes like a wave.
- **1865 · James Clerk Maxwell** shows light is an electromagnetic wave.
- **1873 · Ernst Abbe** finds the resolution limit of microscopes.
- **1960 · Theodore Maiman** builds the first laser.
- **1966 · Charles Kao** proposes low-loss optical fibres.
- **1990s · Akasaki, Amano and Nakamura** make efficient blue LEDs.

# What you will be able to do

At the end of this path you will know optics the way a strong undergraduate course expects. You will be able to:

- **Use ray optics:** reflection, refraction, lenses, mirrors and optical instruments.
- **Use wave optics:** interference, diffraction, polarisation and resolution limits.
- **Explain light and matter:** spectra, emission and absorption, lasers and LEDs.
- **Explain photonic technologies:** fibres, holography, solar cells and optical tweezers.
- **Discuss frontiers:** metamaterials, interferometry, light pollution and research.
- **For the Mastery certificate:** have written the Scholar challenges and passed the final mastery check.

These goals are a draft standard. Physicists and optical engineers from more than one country will confirm them before the path is published.
