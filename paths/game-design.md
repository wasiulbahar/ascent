# Game design

How games are designed and why we play: the nature of play, the fundamentals of game design, making your own games with free tools, games as culture and argument, board games and stories, and the industry and its ethics. About 250 to 300 hours over ten to twelve months, with plenty of making and playtesting.

**Access and balance.** Every title is labelled **Free**, **Free + paid** or **Paid**. Free game engines, tools, video essays and research libraries cover much of the path, and paper prototypes cost almost nothing. Every stage asks you to make and test a game. Board games and games from many countries are included alongside video games.

# About game design

Game design is the craft of creating the rules, goals, choices, challenges and feedback that make a game, and the experience players have. It covers board, card and video games, and draws on psychology, mathematics, storytelling, art, programming and economics. Game studies looks at what play means for people and culture.

This path begins with books on why play matters, teaches the fundamentals of game design, gets you making games with free tools, reads the theory of play and games as culture, widens to board games and interactive stories, and ends with the industry and the ethics of games today.

**Trivia**
- The Dutch historian Johan Huizinga argued in 1938 that play is older than culture, since animals play too.
- *Spacewar!*, made at MIT in 1962, is one of the earliest video games; *Pong* (1972) was among the first commercial hits.
- Chess descends from *chaturanga*, played in India around the sixth century CE, and mancala games are among the oldest board games known.
- Belgium decided in 2018 that some paid loot boxes in video games count as illegal gambling.

### Timeline
- **c. 3000 BCE · The Royal Game of Ur** and senet in Egypt.
- **c. 6th century CE · Chaturanga** in India, the ancestor of chess.
- **1938 · Huizinga's *Homo Ludens***
- **1962 · *Spacewar!*** at MIT.
- **1972 · *Pong*** and the Magnavox Odyssey.
- **1974 · *Dungeons & Dragons***
- **1980s · Japan's console era** Nintendo and the games of Shigeru Miyamoto.
- **1995 · *The Settlers of Catan*** revives board games.
- **2000s · Independent games** and digital distribution.
- **2010s · Mobile games** reach billions of players.

# What you will be able to do

At the end of this path you will know game design the way a strong first year of a university game-design course expects. You will be able to:

- **Explain play:** why people play and what makes games fun.
- **Use design frameworks:** mechanics, dynamics and aesthetics, and design lenses.
- **Make and test games:** paper prototypes, interactive stories and a simple digital game.
- **Analyse games as culture:** games that argue, tell stories and reflect societies.
- **Weigh the ethics:** loot boxes, crunch, accessibility and design for good.
- **For the Mastery certificate:** have written the Scholar challenges and passed the final mastery check.

These goals are a draft standard. Game designers and game scholars from more than one continent will confirm them before the path is published.

**How stages work.** Each work ends with a short quiz. Score 70% or more across a stage's quizzes to pass it. If you fall short, you can retake the quizzes, or continue anyway: the stage then stays marked **Not finished** until you come back and pass it.

# Final mastery check

Finishing all six stages makes you a capable student of game design. To earn the **Ascent Mastery: Game design** certificate, you also complete:

1. **The grand quiz.** 40 game-style questions across all six stages. Pass mark 80%, with unlimited retakes.
2. **Design portfolio.** A paper game, an interactive story and a small digital game, each with playtest notes.
3. **Game analysis.** Analyse an unseen game using MDA and Schell's lenses.
4. **Capstone essay.** 2,500 words on one of the Stage 6 questions. Strong essays are read by an expert reviewer.
5. **Explain it.** Answer three unseen questions as if to a curious friend. For example: “Why are some games so hard to stop playing?”

The certificate needs every stage passed.
