# Nutrition

Nutrition studies how food nourishes the body: energy, proteins, fats, carbohydrates, vitamins and minerals, and how diet affects health, growth and disease. Dietetics applies it to people's care.

## Key works
- beginner · *Food Rules* · Michael Pollan · 2009 · paid | Short, memorable rules for eating well.
- beginner · *Ultra-Processed People* · Chris van Tulleken · 2023 · paid | How ultra-processed food affects health.
- intermediate · *Nutrition Science and Everyday Application* · Alice Callahan, Heather Leonard and Tamberly Powell · 2020 · free | A free open textbook.
- intermediate · Healthy diet fact sheet · World Health Organization · ongoing · free | Free, plain guidance for the whole world.
- advanced · *Modern Nutrition in Health and Disease* · A. Catharine Ross and others · latest edition · paid | The standard advanced reference.
