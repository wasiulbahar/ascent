# Artificial intelligence

Artificial intelligence builds computer systems that perform tasks we associate with intelligence: recognising images, understanding language, making decisions. Modern AI mostly learns from data, which brings both striking abilities and new risks.

## Key works
- beginner · *Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans* · Melanie Mitchell · 2019 · paid | A clear, balanced account of what AI can and can't do.
- beginner · *The Alignment Problem* · Brian Christian · 2020 · paid | How to make AI systems do what we actually want.
- intermediate · Neural networks (video series) · Grant Sanderson (3Blue1Brown) · 2017 onwards · free | Free animated explanations of how neural networks learn.
- intermediate · *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach* · Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig · 4th edition 2020 · paid | The standard textbook (also in the Computer science path).
- advanced · *Deep Learning* · Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio and Aaron Courville · 2016 · free | The standard deep-learning text, free to read online.
