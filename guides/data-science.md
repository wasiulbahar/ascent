# Data science

Data science combines statistics, programming and knowledge of a subject to turn data into understanding and decisions. It includes collecting and cleaning data, visualising it, building models, and thinking carefully about bias and fairness.

## Key works
- beginner · *Factfulness* · Hans Rosling · 2018 · paid | How data can correct our view of the world.
- beginner · *Hello World* · Hannah Fry · 2018 · paid | How algorithms already make decisions about us.
- intermediate · *R for Data Science* · Hadley Wickham and Garrett Grolemund · 2017 (2nd edition 2023) · free | A free online book on the whole data workflow in R.
- intermediate · *Python Data Science Handbook* · Jake VanderPlas · 2016 · free | A free online book on data tools in Python.
- advanced · *The Elements of Statistical Learning* · Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani and Jerome Friedman · 2nd edition 2009 · free | The classic advanced text, free as a PDF from the authors.
