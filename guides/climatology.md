# Climatology

Climatology studies climate: the long-term patterns of temperature, rain and wind, what drives them, and how they change. Today its central question is how greenhouse gases from human activity are warming the planet, and what that means for people and ecosystems.

## Key works
- beginner · *Climate Change: A Very Short Introduction* · Mark Maslin · 4th edition 2021 · paid | A clear, compact overview of the science.
- beginner · *Field Notes from a Catastrophe* · Elizabeth Kolbert · 2006 · paid | Reporting from the places where climate change is already visible.
- intermediate · Global Warming: The Science and Modeling of Climate Change · David Archer, University of Chicago · ongoing · free | A free online course with simple climate models.
- intermediate · Summary for Policymakers, Sixth Assessment Report · Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) · 2021 · free | The scientific consensus, summarised by the world's climate scientists.
- advanced · *Global Warming: Understanding the Forecast* · David Archer · 2nd edition 2011 · paid | The textbook that goes with the course.
