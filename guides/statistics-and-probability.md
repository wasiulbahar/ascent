# Statistics

Statistics is the science of learning from data: describing it, finding patterns, and judging how sure we can be. Probability is its language for uncertainty. Together they underpin medicine, polling, science and machine learning.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Art of Statistics* · David Spiegelhalter · 2019 · paid | How to learn from data, told through real questions.
- beginner · *Naked Statistics* · Charles Wheelan · 2013 · paid | A funny, friendly introduction to the key ideas.
- intermediate · Statistics and probability · Khan Academy · ongoing · free | Free lessons and practice.
- intermediate · *OpenIntro Statistics* · David Diez, Mine Çetinkaya-Rundel and Christopher Barr · 4th edition 2019 · free | A free university textbook.
- advanced · *Statistical Inference* · George Casella and Roger Berger · 2nd edition 2002 · paid | The standard advanced theory text.
