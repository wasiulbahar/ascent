# Pharmacy

Pharmacy is the science and practice of preparing, checking and supplying medicines and advising on their safe use. Pharmacists connect the chemistry of drugs with the people who take them.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Drug Hunters* · Donald R. Kirsch and Ogi Ogas · 2017 · paid | How new medicines have been found, from plants to laboratories.
- beginner · *Bad Pharma* · Ben Goldacre · 2012 · paid | How evidence about medicines can be distorted.
- intermediate · *Pharmacology* · OpenStax · 2024 · free | A free open textbook on how drugs work.
- intermediate · *WHO Model List of Essential Medicines* · World Health Organization · updated every two years · free | The medicines a basic health system needs, and why.
- advanced · *Remington: The Science and Practice of Pharmacy* · Adeboye Adejare (editor) · 23rd edition 2020 · paid | The classic reference on pharmacy.
