# Criminology

Criminology studies crime: what counts as crime, why people offend, how victims are affected, and how police, courts and prisons respond. It asks which responses actually reduce harm.

## Key works
- beginner · *Crime: A Very Short Introduction* · Tim Newburn · 2018 · paid | A short overview of the field.
- beginner · *Just Mercy* · Bryan Stevenson · 2014 · paid | A lawyer on the failures of criminal justice.
- intermediate · *Outsiders* · Howard Becker · 1963 · paid | A classic study of how behaviour gets labelled deviant.
- intermediate · Global Study on Homicide · UN Office on Drugs and Crime (UNODC) · regularly · free | Free world data on violent crime.
- advanced · *The Oxford Handbook of Criminology* · Alison Liebling, Shadd Maruna and Lesley McAra (editors) · latest edition · paid | The standard advanced survey.
