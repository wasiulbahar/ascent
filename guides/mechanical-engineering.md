# Mechanical engineering

Mechanical engineering designs machines and anything that moves or transfers energy: engines, turbines, pumps, vehicles, robots and the parts inside them. It rests on mechanics, materials, thermodynamics and design.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Way Things Work Now* · David Macaulay · 2016 · paid | An illustrated guide to how machines work, loved by all ages.
- beginner · *The Secret Life of Machines* · Tim Hunkin and Rex Garrod · 1988–1993 · free | A witty television series on everyday machines, free on the presenter's website.
- intermediate · *Engineering Mechanics: Statics* · J. L. Meriam and L. G. Kraige · latest edition · paid | The standard first course in forces and equilibrium.
- intermediate · 2.001 Mechanics and Materials I · MIT OpenCourseWare · ongoing · free | A free university course.
- advanced · *Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design* · Richard Budynas and Keith Nisbett · latest edition · paid | The standard text on designing machine parts.
