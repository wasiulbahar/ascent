# Genetics

Genetics is the study of heredity: how traits pass from parents to offspring, how genes are built from DNA, and how they are switched on and off. It began with Gregor Mendel's pea plants in the 1860s, became molecular with the discovery of DNA's structure in 1953, and today reads whole genomes in hours. It underpins medicine, agriculture and the study of evolution.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Gene: An Intimate History* · Siddhartha Mukherjee · 2016 · paid | A history of the gene from Mendel to gene editing.
- beginner · *Genome* · Matt Ridley · 1999 · paid | One short chapter for each human chromosome.
- intermediate · Heredity and genetics · Khan Academy · ongoing · free | Free videos and practice on Mendelian genetics and DNA.
- intermediate · 7.03 Genetics · MIT OpenCourseWare · 2004 · free | A full university course with problem sets.
- advanced · *Introduction to Genetic Analysis* · Anthony Griffiths and others · latest edition · paid | The standard textbook, built around how geneticists find things out.
