# Entrepreneurship

Entrepreneurship is starting and growing new ventures, from small shops to technology start-ups and social enterprises. It involves spotting opportunities, testing ideas, raising money and managing risk.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Lean Startup* · Eric Ries · 2011 · paid | Build, measure, learn: testing ideas cheaply.
- beginner · *Zero to One* · Peter Thiel with Blake Masters · 2014 · paid | A provocative case for building something new.
- intermediate · *Business Model Generation* · Alexander Osterwalder and Yves Pigneur · 2010 · paid | The Business Model Canvas.
- intermediate · Entrepreneurship courses · MIT OpenCourseWare · ongoing · free | Free university courses.
- advanced · *Innovation and Entrepreneurship* · Peter Drucker · 1985 · paid | Drucker's systematic view of where opportunities come from.

## Quick check
**Multiple choice.** What is a minimum viable product?
- The simplest version that lets you learn from real customers ✓
- A perfect finished product
- The cheapest product

**Multiple choice.** What is the Business Model Canvas?
- A one-page tool to describe how a business creates and captures value ✓
- An oil painting
- A tax form

**Multiple choice.** What is a social enterprise?
- A business whose main aim is a social or environmental goal ✓
- A party
- A government ministry
