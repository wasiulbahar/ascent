# Graphic design

Graphic design communicates with type, image, colour and layout: posters, books, logos, signs, websites and apps. It is everywhere, and good design makes information clear.

## Key works
- beginner · *Thinking with Type* · Ellen Lupton · 3rd edition 2024 · paid | The standard friendly guide to typography.
- beginner · *Graphic Design: A New History* · Stephen Eskilson · 3rd edition 2019 · paid | How graphic design developed.
- intermediate · *The Non-Designer's Design Book* · Robin Williams · 4th edition 2014 · paid | Four basic principles: contrast, repetition, alignment and proximity.
- intermediate · Practical Typography · Matthew Butterick · ongoing · free | A free online book on using type well.
- advanced · *The Elements of Typographic Style* · Robert Bringhurst · 1992 (4th edition 2012) · paid | The classic on typography.
