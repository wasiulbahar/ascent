# Neurobiology

Neurobiology studies the nervous system: how nerve cells send electrical and chemical signals, how they connect into circuits, and how those circuits produce movement, senses, memory and behaviour. It links cell biology, chemistry and psychology.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat* · Oliver Sacks · 1985 · paid | Case stories that show what the brain does by what happens when it fails.
- beginner · *The Brain: The Story of You* · David Eagleman · 2015 · paid | A short, popular tour of the brain.
- intermediate · 9.01 Introduction to Neuroscience · MIT OpenCourseWare · 2007 · free | A university introduction with lecture notes.
- intermediate · *Neuroscience: Exploring the Brain* · Mark Bear, Barry Connors and Michael Paradiso · latest edition · paid | A clear, well-illustrated undergraduate textbook.
- advanced · *Principles of Neural Science* · Eric Kandel and others · latest edition · paid | The standard advanced reference.
