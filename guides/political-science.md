# Political science

Political science studies power and government: how states are organised, how people take part in politics, how policies are made, and how countries deal with each other. It asks what makes governments legitimate, stable and fair.

## Key works
- beginner · *In Defence of Politics* · Bernard Crick · 1962 · paid | A short classic on why politics, with all its compromises, matters.
- beginner · *The Origins of Political Order* · Francis Fukuyama · 2011 · paid | How states, law and accountability developed around the world.
- intermediate · Introduction to Political Philosophy (PLSC 114) · Steven B. Smith, Open Yale Courses · 2006 · free | A free course on the great political thinkers.
- intermediate · *Politics* · Andrew Heywood · latest edition · paid | A widely used introductory textbook.
- advanced · *Polyarchy* · Robert Dahl · 1971 · paid | A classic study of what makes democracy possible.
