# Demography

Demography studies populations: births, deaths, migration, ageing and how they change. It explains why some countries are young and growing while others are old and shrinking.

## Key works
- beginner · *Factfulness* · Hans Rosling · 2018 · paid | How the world's population is really changing.
- beginner · *The Human Tide* · Paul Morland · 2019 · paid | How population shaped the modern world.
- intermediate · *World Population Prospects* · United Nations · regularly · free | The UN's free population estimates and projections.
- intermediate · Our World in Data: Population · Our World in Data · ongoing · free | Free charts and explanations.
- advanced · *Demography: Measuring and Modeling Population Processes* · Samuel Preston, Patrick Heuveline and Michel Guillot · 2001 · paid | The standard methods text.

## Quick check
**Multiple choice.** What is the fertility rate?
- The average number of children per woman ✓
- The number of farms
- The speed of growth of plants

**Multiple choice.** What is the demographic transition?
- The shift from high births and deaths to low births and deaths ✓
- Moving house
- A kind of census

**Multiple choice.** Why are many countries ageing?
- People live longer and have fewer children ✓
- Calendars changed
- Nobody knows
