# Epidemiology

Epidemiology studies how diseases spread and what causes them in populations. It counts cases, compares groups and traces outbreaks, and it guides public health, from clean water to vaccines and pandemic planning.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Ghost Map* · Steven Johnson · 2006 · paid | John Snow and the 1854 London cholera outbreak.
- beginner · *The Rules of Contagion* · Adam Kucharski · 2020 · paid | How outbreaks, and ideas, spread.
- intermediate · *Principles of Epidemiology in Public Health Practice* · US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention · 3rd edition · free | A free self-study course.
- intermediate · *Gordis Epidemiology* · Leon Gordis and others · latest edition · paid | A clear introductory textbook.
- advanced · *Modern Epidemiology* · Kenneth Rothman, Sander Greenland and Timothy Lash · latest edition · paid | The standard advanced text.

## Quick check

- By mapping cases around a water pump ✓

**Multiple choice.** What does R, the reproduction number, describe?

- The death rate

**Multiple choice.** What is the difference between incidence and prevalence?
- Incidence counts new cases; prevalence counts all existing cases ✓
- They are the same
- Prevalence counts only deaths
