# Accounting

Accounting records and reports a business's money: what it owns, owes, earns and spends. It is the language of business and the basis for decisions, taxes and trust.

## Key works
- beginner · *Double Entry* · Jane Gleeson-White · 2011 · paid | How Pacioli's bookkeeping shaped the modern world.
- beginner · *Financial Intelligence* · Karen Berman and Joe Knight · 2006 (revised 2013) · paid | Reading financial statements for non-specialists.
- intermediate · *Principles of Accounting, Volume 1* · OpenStax · 2019 · free | A free textbook on financial accounting.
- intermediate · International Financial Reporting Standards (summaries) · IFRS Foundation · ongoing · free | Free summaries of the standards used in many countries.
- advanced · *Intermediate Accounting* · Donald Kieso, Jerry Weygandt and Terry Warfield · latest edition · paid | The standard advanced textbook.
