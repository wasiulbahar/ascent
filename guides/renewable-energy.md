# Renewable energy

Renewable energy comes from sources that are replaced naturally: sunlight, wind, water, heat from the Earth and plants. Switching to it is central to cutting carbon emissions.

## Key works
- beginner · *Sustainable Energy – Without the Hot Air* · David MacKay · 2008 · free | Clear numbers on energy, free online from the author.
- beginner · *How to Avoid a Climate Disaster* · Bill Gates · 2021 · paid | A plain overview of cutting emissions.
- intermediate · *World Energy Outlook* · International Energy Agency · yearly · free-and-paid | The main annual report; summaries are free.
- intermediate · Renewable energy statistics · International Renewable Energy Agency (IRENA) · yearly · free | Free data on renewables worldwide.
- advanced · *Renewable Energy: Power for a Sustainable Future* · Stephen Peake (editor) · 4th edition 2018 · paid | A standard textbook.
