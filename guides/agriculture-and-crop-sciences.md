# Agriculture

Agriculture studies how people grow food and raise animals: soils, crops, breeding, water, pests and farming systems, and how to feed a growing world without exhausting land, water and climate.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Omnivore's Dilemma* · Michael Pollan · 2006 · paid | Where our food comes from, followed through four meals.
- beginner · *The Wizard and the Prophet* · Charles C. Mann · 2018 · paid | Two visions of feeding the world: high-tech farming or living within limits.
- intermediate · *An Edible History of Humanity* · Tom Standage · 2009 · paid | How food and farming shaped history.
- intermediate · *The State of Food and Agriculture* · Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO) · yearly · free | Free yearly reports on world farming and food.
- advanced · *Agroecology: The Ecology of Sustainable Food Systems* · Stephen Gliessman · latest edition · paid | The standard text on ecological farming.
