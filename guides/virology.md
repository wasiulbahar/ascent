# Virology

Viruses are tiny packages of genetic material that can only copy themselves inside living cells. Virology studies how they infect, spread and evolve, how bodies fight them, and how vaccines and antiviral drugs work. Viruses also shape ecosystems and have left traces in our own DNA.

## Key works
- beginner · *A Planet of Viruses* · Carl Zimmer · 2011 · paid | Short essays on the viruses around and inside us.
- beginner · *Viruses: A Very Short Introduction* · Dorothy H. Crawford · latest edition · paid | A compact overview of viruses and disease.
- intermediate · Virology lectures · Vincent Racaniello, Columbia University · ongoing · free | A full university virology course, free online.
- intermediate · *Principles of Molecular Virology* · Alan J. Cann · 6th edition 2015 · paid | A readable textbook on how viruses work.
- advanced · *Principles of Virology* · Jane Flint and others · latest edition · paid | The standard advanced textbook.
