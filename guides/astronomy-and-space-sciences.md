# Astronomy and space sciences

Astronomy studies everything beyond Earth: planets, stars, galaxies and the universe as a whole. It is the oldest science, born from watching the sky, and today it uses telescopes on the ground and in space to read light that has travelled for billions of years.

## Key works
- beginner · *Cosmos* · Carl Sagan · 1980 · paid | The classic tour of the universe and our place in it.
- beginner · *Astrophysics for People in a Hurry* · Neil deGrasse Tyson · 2017 · paid | Short chapters on the big ideas.
- intermediate · Crash Course Astronomy · Phil Plait · 2015 · free | Free short videos covering the whole subject.
- intermediate · *Astronomy 2e* · OpenStax · 2022 · free | A complete free university textbook.
- advanced · *An Introduction to Modern Astrophysics* · Bradley Carroll and Dale Ostlie · 2nd edition 2007 · paid | The standard astrophysics textbook.
