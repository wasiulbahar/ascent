# Ecology

Ecology studies how living things interact with each other and with their surroundings: food webs, populations, communities and whole ecosystems. It explains why species thrive or vanish, and underpins conservation.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Sixth Extinction* · Elizabeth Kolbert · 2014 · paid | How humans are driving a mass extinction; it won the Pulitzer Prize.
- beginner · *Entangled Life* · Merlin Sheldrake · 2020 · paid | The hidden world of fungi that connect ecosystems.
- intermediate · Ecology · Khan Academy · ongoing · free | Free lessons on populations, communities and ecosystems.
- intermediate · *Ecology: Concepts and Applications* · Manuel Molles · latest edition · paid | A clear introductory textbook.
- advanced · *Ecology: From Individuals to Ecosystems* · Michael Begon and Colin Townsend · latest edition · paid | The standard advanced textbook.

## Quick check
**Multiple choice.** What is a food web?
- The network of who eats whom in an ecosystem ✓
- A spider's web
- A shopping list

**Multiple choice.** What is a keystone species?
- A species with a much larger effect on its ecosystem than its numbers suggest ✓
- The largest species
- A species of stone

**Multiple choice.** What does *The Sixth Extinction* argue?
- Humans are driving a mass extinction ✓
- No species have gone extinct
- Extinction is impossible
