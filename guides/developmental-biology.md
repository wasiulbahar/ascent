# Developmental biology

How does a single fertilised egg become a whole body with a head, limbs and a beating heart? Developmental biology studies how cells divide, specialise and organise themselves into tissues and organs, and how the same small set of genes builds very different animals.

## Key works
- beginner · *Your Inner Fish* · Neil Shubin · 2008 · paid | How the human body carries the history of evolution.
- beginner · *The Triumph of the Embryo* · Lewis Wolpert · 1991 · paid | A short, lively account of how embryos develop.
- intermediate · *Endless Forms Most Beautiful* · Sean B. Carroll · 2005 · paid | How the same "toolkit" genes build different animals.
- intermediate · iBiology talks on development · iBiology · ongoing · free | Free video talks by leading developmental biologists.
- advanced · *Developmental Biology* · Scott Gilbert and Michael Barresi · latest edition · paid | The standard university textbook.
