# Gender studies

Gender studies examines how ideas of gender shape lives, societies, power and knowledge, and how gender interacts with class, race, sexuality and other parts of identity.

## Key works
- beginner · *We Should All Be Feminists* · Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie · 2014 · paid | A short, famous essay adapted from a talk.
- beginner · *Invisible Women* · Caroline Criado Perez · 2019 · paid | How data gaps leave women out of design and policy.
- intermediate · *The Second Sex* · Simone de Beauvoir · 1949 · paid | The classic: “One is not born, but rather becomes, a woman.”
- intermediate · Global Gender Gap Report · World Economic Forum · yearly · free | Free data on gender gaps in many countries.
- advanced · *Gender Trouble* · Judith Butler · 1990 · paid | An influential and difficult theory of gender as performative.
