# Game design

Game design is the craft of making games: their rules, goals, choices and feedback, and the experience they create for players, from board games to video games.

## Key works
- beginner · *A Theory of Fun for Game Design* · Raph Koster · 2004 (2nd edition 2013) · paid | A short, illustrated book on why games are fun: learning patterns.
- beginner · *Blood, Sweat, and Pixels* · Jason Schreier · 2017 · paid | Behind the scenes of how ten video games were made.
- intermediate · *The Art of Game Design: A Book of Lenses* · Jesse Schell · 3rd edition 2019 · paid | Over a hundred “lenses”, questions to look at a design through.
- intermediate · Game Maker's Toolkit · Mark Brown · 2014 onwards · free | Free video essays on how game mechanics work.
- advanced · *Rules of Play* · Katie Salen and Eric Zimmerman · 2003 · paid | The standard academic text on game design.

## Quick check
**Multiple choice.** What does Raph Koster say makes games fun?
- Learning and mastering patterns ✓
- Winning prizes
- Loud sounds

**Multiple choice.** What is a game mechanic?
- A rule or action that players use, such as jumping or trading ✓
- A repair person
- A game's price

**Multiple choice.** Why do designers playtest?
- To see how real players experience the game ✓
- To advertise it
- To finish faster
