# Epigenetics

Every cell in your body carries the same DNA, yet a nerve cell and a skin cell are very different. Epigenetics studies the chemical marks and packaging that switch genes on and off without changing the DNA sequence itself, and how some of those patterns can last through cell divisions.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Epigenetics Revolution* · Nessa Carey · 2011 · paid | A clear, popular introduction by a molecular biologist.
- beginner · *Identically Different* · Tim Spector · 2012 · paid | What studies of identical twins reveal about genes and environment.
- intermediate · Epigenetics · Learn.Genetics, University of Utah · ongoing · free | Free illustrated lessons and animations.
- intermediate · *Molecular Biology of the Cell*, chapter on gene regulation · Bruce Alberts and others · 7th edition 2022 · paid | The textbook account of how genes are controlled.
- advanced · *Epigenetics* · C. David Allis and others (eds.) · 2nd edition 2015 · paid | The standard advanced text from Cold Spring Harbor.
