# Theology

Theology studies religious belief from within a tradition: ideas about God or the divine, scripture, ethics and practice, using reason, history and interpretation. Many traditions have their own theology.

## Key works
- beginner · *Mere Christianity* · C. S. Lewis · 1952 · paid | A clear, popular case for Christian belief.
- beginner · *The Heart of Islamic Philosophy* · William C. Chittick · 2001 · paid | An introduction to Islamic thought through the writings of Afdal al-Din Kashani.
- intermediate · *Theology: A Very Short Introduction* · David F. Ford · 1999 (2nd edition 2013) · paid | A short map of what theology is and does.
- intermediate · *The Guide for the Perplexed* · Moses Maimonides · c. 1190 · free | A great work of Jewish philosophy, in free older translations.
- advanced · *Summa Theologiae* (selections) · Thomas Aquinas · 1265–1274 · free | Medieval Christian theology at its most systematic, free online.
