# Human rights

Human rights are the rights everyone has because they are human: to life, liberty, equality, expression, education and more. The field studies their history, law, philosophy and how they are protected or violated.

## Key works
- beginner · Universal Declaration of Human Rights · United Nations · 1948 · free | Thirty articles, free in hundreds of languages.
- beginner · *Inventing Human Rights* · Lynn Hunt · 2007 · paid | How the idea of human rights arose.
- intermediate · *The Last Utopia* · Samuel Moyn · 2010 · paid | Argues that human rights became a mass movement only in the 1970s.
- intermediate · World Report · Human Rights Watch · yearly · free | Free yearly reports on human rights in about 100 countries.
- advanced · *International Human Rights* · Philip Alston and Ryan Goodman · 2013 · paid | The standard advanced legal text.
