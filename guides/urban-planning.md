# Urban planning

Urban planning shapes how cities grow: where homes, jobs, streets, parks and transport go, and who benefits. More than half the world's people now live in cities, so planning decides much of daily life.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Death and Life of Great American Cities* · Jane Jacobs · 1961 · paid | A classic attack on top-down planning and a defence of lively streets.
- beginner · *Triumph of the City* · Edward Glaeser · 2011 · paid | An economist on why cities make us richer and greener.
- intermediate · *Cities for People* · Jan Gehl · 2010 · paid | Designing cities around people on foot.
- intermediate · *World Cities Report* · UN-Habitat · regularly · free | Free reports on cities around the world.
- advanced · *Cities of Tomorrow* · Peter Hall · 4th edition 2014 · paid | A history of planning ideas in the twentieth century.
