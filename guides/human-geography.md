# Human geography

Human geography studies how people live across the Earth: where they settle, how they move, how cities, economies and cultures differ from place to place, and how people shape and are shaped by their environment.

## Key works
- beginner · *Prisoners of Geography* · Tim Marshall · 2015 · paid | How maps explain world politics.
- beginner · *Guns, Germs, and Steel* · Jared Diamond · 1997 · paid | A bold argument about geography and history; read with its critics.
- intermediate · *Introduction to Human Geography* · David Dorrell and Joseph Henderson · 2nd edition 2019 · free | A free open textbook.
- intermediate · *Human Geography: A Very Short Introduction* · Andrew Harris and others · latest edition · paid | A short guide to the field and its ideas.
- advanced · *The Dictionary of Human Geography* · Derek Gregory and others · 5th edition 2009 · paid | The standard reference for key ideas.
