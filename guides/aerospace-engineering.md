# Aerospace engineering

Aerospace engineering designs aircraft, spacecraft, satellites and rockets. It combines aerodynamics, propulsion, structures, materials and control so that machines can fly safely through the air and into space.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Wright Brothers* · David McCullough · 2015 · paid | How two bicycle makers solved the problem of powered flight.
- beginner · *Ignition!* · John D. Clark · 1972 · paid | A funny insider history of rocket fuels.
- intermediate · *Introduction to Flight* · John D. Anderson Jr. · latest edition · paid | The standard first textbook on how aircraft fly.
- intermediate · Beginner's Guide to Aeronautics · NASA Glenn Research Center · ongoing · free | Free illustrated pages on lift, drag, thrust and weight.
- advanced · *Fundamentals of Astrodynamics* · Roger Bate, Donald Mueller and Jerry White · 1971 (2nd edition 2020) · paid | A classic text on orbits.
