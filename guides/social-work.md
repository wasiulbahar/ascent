# Social work

Social work helps people and communities cope with problems such as poverty, abuse, illness, disability and exclusion. It combines practical help with a commitment to social justice and human rights.

## Key works
- beginner · *Evicted* · Matthew Desmond · 2016 · paid | How eviction traps families in poverty.
- beginner · *The Body Keeps the Score* · Bessel van der Kolk · 2014 · paid | How trauma affects people; widely read by social workers, though debated.
- intermediate · Global Definition of Social Work · International Federation of Social Workers · 2014 · free | The profession's own definition, free online.
- intermediate · *Social Work: A Very Short Introduction* · Sally Holland and Jonathan Scourfield · 2015 · paid | A short overview of social work around the world.
- advanced · *Modern Social Work Theory* · Malcolm Payne · 5th edition 2021 · paid | The standard text on theories of practice.
