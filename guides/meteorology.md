# Meteorology

Meteorology is the science of the atmosphere and weather: air pressure, wind, clouds, storms and how forecasts are made with observations and computer models.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Cloudspotter's Guide* · Gavin Pretor-Pinney · 2006 · paid | A joyful guide to clouds.
- beginner · *The Weather Machine* · Andrew Blum · 2019 · paid | How the global forecasting system works.
- intermediate · Learn about weather (JetStream) · US National Weather Service · ongoing · free | A free online school for weather.
- intermediate · *Meteorology Today* · C. Donald Ahrens and Robert Henson · latest edition · paid | A standard introductory textbook.
- advanced · *An Introduction to Dynamic Meteorology* · James Holton and Gregory Hakim · 5th edition 2013 · paid | The standard advanced text.
