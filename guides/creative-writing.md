# Creative writing

Creative writing is the practice of writing fiction, poetry, drama and creative non-fiction. It is learned by reading closely, writing often and revising hard.

## Key works
- beginner · *On Writing* · Stephen King · 2000 · paid | Part memoir, part practical advice from a hugely popular novelist.
- beginner · *Bird by Bird* · Anne Lamott · 1994 · paid | Warm, funny advice on getting words on the page.
- intermediate · *A Swim in a Pond in the Rain* · George Saunders · 2021 · paid | A writing class built on reading seven Russian short stories.
- intermediate · International Writing Program courses · University of Iowa · ongoing · free | Free online courses in fiction and poetry.
- advanced · *The Art of Fiction* · John Gardner · 1983 · paid | A demanding classic for serious fiction writers.
