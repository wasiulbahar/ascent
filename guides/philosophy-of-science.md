# Philosophy of science

Philosophy of science asks what science is, how it differs from other ways of knowing, how evidence supports theories, and how science changes over time.

## Key works
- beginner · *What Is This Thing Called Science?* · Alan Chalmers · 1976 (4th edition 2013) · paid | The classic introduction.
- beginner · *The Knowledge Machine* · Michael Strevens · 2020 · paid | Why science works, and why it took so long to begin.
- intermediate · *The Logic of Scientific Discovery* · Karl Popper · 1934 (English 1959) · paid | Falsifiability as the mark of science.
- intermediate · Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy · Stanford University · ongoing · free | Free expert articles on every topic in the field.
- advanced · *The Structure of Scientific Revolutions* · Thomas Kuhn · 1962 · paid | Paradigms and paradigm shifts.

## Quick check
**Multiple choice.** What did Popper say marks a theory as scientific?
- It can be falsified by evidence ✓
- It is popular
- It is old

**Multiple choice.** What is a paradigm shift, in Kuhn's sense?
- A revolution in the basic framework of a science ✓
- A change of government
- A new dance

**Multiple choice.** What is induction?
- Reasoning from particular cases to general rules ✓
- Turning on a machine
- Starting a job
