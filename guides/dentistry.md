# Dentistry

Dentistry studies and treats the teeth, gums and mouth. Oral diseases such as tooth decay and gum disease are among the most common diseases in the world.

## Key works
- beginner · Oral health fact sheet · World Health Organization · ongoing · free | Free facts on oral disease worldwide.
- beginner · *Teeth: The Story of Beauty, Inequality, and the Struggle for Oral Health in America* · Mary Otto · 2017 · paid | Oral health and inequality.
- intermediate · *Oral Anatomy, Histology and Embryology* · Barry Berkovitz, Bernard Moxham and others · latest edition · paid | A standard foundation text.
- intermediate · Global oral health status report · World Health Organization · 2022 · free | Free global data on oral disease.
- advanced · *Sturdevant's Art and Science of Operative Dentistry* · André Ritter (editor) and others · latest edition · paid | A standard clinical text.
