# Civil engineering

Civil engineering designs and builds the structures societies depend on: bridges, buildings, roads, railways, dams, water supply and sewers. It applies physics and materials science so that structures stay safe for decades.

## Key works
- beginner · *Built* · Roma Agrawal · 2018 · paid | The hidden stories behind famous structures, by a structural engineer.
- beginner · *To Engineer Is Human* · Henry Petroski · 1985 · paid | Why failures teach engineers how to succeed.
- intermediate · *Structures: Or Why Things Don't Fall Down* · J. E. Gordon · 1978 · paid | A classic, witty explanation of how structures carry loads.
- intermediate · Civil and Environmental Engineering courses · MIT OpenCourseWare · ongoing · free | Free university courses on mechanics and structures.
- advanced · *Structural Analysis* · Russell C. Hibbeler · latest edition · paid | A standard university textbook.
