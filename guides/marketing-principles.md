# Marketing

Marketing is how organisations understand what people need, create offers for them, and communicate and deliver those offers. It covers research, branding, pricing, advertising and digital channels.

## Key works
- beginner · *This Is Marketing* · Seth Godin · 2018 · paid | Marketing as serving a specific audience well.
- beginner · *Influence* · Robert Cialdini · 1984 (new edition 2021) · paid | Six principles of persuasion.
- intermediate · *Principles of Marketing* · OpenStax · 2023 · free | A free open textbook.
- intermediate · *How Brands Grow* · Byron Sharp · 2010 · paid | Evidence on how brands actually win buyers.
- advanced · *Marketing Management* · Philip Kotler and Kevin Lane Keller · latest edition · paid | The standard advanced text.
