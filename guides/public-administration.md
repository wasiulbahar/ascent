# Public administration

Public administration studies how governments actually work: how public services are organised, funded, staffed and held to account, and how policies turn into action.

## Key works
- beginner · *Bureaucracy* · James Q. Wilson · 1989 · paid | Why government agencies do what they do.
- beginner · *Seeing Like a State* · James C. Scott · 1998 · paid | Why big state plans to improve lives often fail.
- intermediate · Worldwide Governance Indicators · World Bank · yearly · free | Free data on how governments perform.
- intermediate · *Introduction to Public Administration* · Jay Shafritz, E. W. Russell and Christopher Borick · latest edition · paid | A standard textbook.
- advanced · *The Oxford Handbook of Public Management* · Ewan Ferlie, Laurence Lynn Jr. and Christopher Pollitt (editors) · 2005 · paid | An advanced survey.
