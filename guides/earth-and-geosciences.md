# Earth sciences

Earth sciences study the planet itself: its rocks, its moving plates, its earthquakes and volcanoes, its oceans and atmosphere, and its 4.5-billion-year history. They explain where mountains, minerals and natural hazards come from.

## Key works
- beginner · *Annals of the Former World* · John McPhee · 1998 · paid | Geology told as a road trip across North America; it won the Pulitzer Prize.
- beginner · *Earth: An Intimate History* · Richard Fortey · 2004 · paid | A palaeontologist's tour of the planet's landscapes.
- intermediate · *Physical Geology* · Steven Earle · 2nd edition 2019 · free | A free open textbook from BCcampus.
- intermediate · 12.001 Introduction to Geology · MIT OpenCourseWare · ongoing · free | A free university course.
- advanced · *Understanding Earth* · John Grotzinger and Thomas Jordan · latest edition · paid | The standard university textbook.
