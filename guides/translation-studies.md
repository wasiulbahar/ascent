# Translation studies

Translation studies looks at how meaning moves between languages: the choices translators make, what is lost and gained, and the role of translation in literature, science, law and power.

## Key works
- beginner · *Is That a Fish in Your Ear?* · David Bellos · 2011 · paid | A lively book on what translation is and isn't.
- beginner · *This Little Art* · Kate Briggs · 2017 · paid | A translator's essay on the art of translating.
- intermediate · *The Translator's Invisibility* · Lawrence Venuti · 1995 (3rd edition 2018) · paid | On “domesticating” versus “foreignising” translation.
- intermediate · UN Terminology Database (UNTERM) · United Nations · ongoing · free | See how official terms are translated across the UN's six languages.
- advanced · *The Translation Studies Reader* · Lawrence Venuti (editor) · 4th edition 2021 · paid | Key texts from across the field.
