# Oceanography

Oceanography studies the ocean, which covers about 71% of Earth's surface: its currents, chemistry, life, sea floor and its role in climate. Much of the deep ocean is still unexplored.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Sea Around Us* · Rachel Carson · 1951 · paid | A classic portrait of the ocean.
- beginner · *The Outlaw Ocean* · Ian Urbina · 2019 · paid | Reporting on lawlessness on the high seas.
- intermediate · *Introduction to Oceanography* · Paul Webb, Roger Williams University · 2019 onwards · free | A free open textbook.
- intermediate · Ocean Explorer · US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) · ongoing · free | Free lessons and live dives from deep-sea expeditions.
- advanced · *Descriptive Physical Oceanography* · Lynne Talley and others · 6th edition 2011 · paid | The standard physical oceanography text.
