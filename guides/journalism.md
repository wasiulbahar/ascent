# Journalism

Journalism gathers, checks and reports news so that citizens can govern themselves. It includes reporting, investigation, editing and verification, and today faces hard questions about trust, business models and misinformation.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Elements of Journalism* · Bill Kovach and Tom Rosenstiel · 2001 · paid | The principles every journalist should know.
- beginner · *Flat Earth News* · Nick Davies · 2008 · paid | An insider's critique of how news goes wrong.
- intermediate · *Verification Handbook* · European Journalism Centre · 2014 · free | A free guide to checking digital content in emergencies.
- intermediate · *Reuters Handbook of Journalism* · Reuters · ongoing · free | A news agency's standards, free online.
- advanced · *The Sociology of News* · Michael Schudson · latest edition · paid | How news is shaped by organisations and society.
