# Theatre

Theatre studies looks at drama in performance: plays, acting, directing, design and audiences, from ancient Greek tragedy and Sanskrit drama to Noh, opera, musicals and experimental work.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Empty Space* · Peter Brook · 1968 · paid | A great director on what theatre is.
- beginner · *The Year of Lear* · James Shapiro · 2015 · paid | Shakespeare's world in the year he wrote three great plays.
- intermediate · National Theatre at Home and other recorded productions · various theatres · ongoing · free-and-paid | Watch great productions; some are free.
- intermediate · *The Natyashastra* · attributed to Bharata · c. 200 BCE–200 CE · free | The ancient Indian treatise on drama, in free translations.
- advanced · *Theatre Histories: An Introduction* · Phillip Zarrilli and others · 3rd edition 2016 · paid | A global history of performance.
