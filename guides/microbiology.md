# Microbiology

Microbiology studies life too small to see: bacteria, archaea, fungi, protists and viruses. Microbes made Earth's oxygen, cause and cure diseases, make bread, cheese and antibiotics, and live in vast numbers in and on every person.

## Key works
- beginner · *I Contain Multitudes* · Ed Yong · 2016 · paid | The microbes that live with animals, including us.
- beginner · *Microbe Hunters* · Paul de Kruif · 1926 · paid | Classic stories of the scientists who discovered microbes, from Leeuwenhoek to Pasteur.
- intermediate · *Microbiology* · OpenStax · 2016 · free | A complete free university textbook.
- intermediate · Microbiology · Khan Academy · ongoing · free | Free lessons on bacteria, viruses and the immune response.
- advanced · *Brock Biology of Microorganisms* · Michael Madigan and others · latest edition · paid | The standard university textbook.
