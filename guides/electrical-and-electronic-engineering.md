# Electrical engineering

Electrical and electronic engineering designs systems that generate, carry and use electricity, from power grids to the circuits inside phones. It covers circuits, electronics, signals, communications and control.

## Key works
- beginner · *Electric Universe* · David Bodanis · 2005 · paid | The story of electricity and the people who tamed it.
- beginner · *Make: Electronics* · Charles Platt · 3rd edition 2021 · paid | Learn electronics by building circuits.
- intermediate · *All About Circuits* · All About Circuits · ongoing · free | A free online textbook on circuits.
- intermediate · 6.002 Circuits and Electronics · MIT OpenCourseWare · ongoing · free | A free university course.
- advanced · *The Art of Electronics* · Paul Horowitz and Winfield Hill · 3rd edition 2015 · paid | The famous practical reference.
