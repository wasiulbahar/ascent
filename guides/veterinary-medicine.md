# Veterinary medicine

Veterinary medicine cares for the health of animals: pets, farm animals and wildlife. It also protects people, since many human diseases come from animals and safe food depends on healthy livestock.

## Key works
- beginner · *All Creatures Great and Small* · James Herriot · 1972 · paid | Warm, funny stories of a country vet in Yorkshire.
- beginner · *Zoobiquity* · Barbara Natterson-Horowitz and Kathryn Bowers · 2012 · paid | What doctors can learn from animal medicine.
- intermediate · One Health · World Organisation for Animal Health (WOAH) · ongoing · free | Free pages on how human, animal and environmental health connect.
- intermediate · *Merck Veterinary Manual* · Merck & Co. · ongoing · free | A free online reference.
- advanced · *Textbook of Veterinary Internal Medicine* · Stephen Ettinger, Edward Feldman and Etienne Côté · latest edition · paid | The standard advanced text.
