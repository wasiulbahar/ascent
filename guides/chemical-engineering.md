# Chemical engineering

Chemical engineering turns raw materials into useful products on a large scale: fuels, plastics, medicines, fertilisers, food and clean water. It designs the processes and plants where chemistry happens safely and efficiently.

## Key works
- beginner · *Stuff Matters* · Mark Miodownik · 2013 · paid | The stories of everyday materials and how they are made.
- beginner · *Napoleon's Buttons* · Penny Le Couteur and Jay Burreson · 2003 · paid | Seventeen molecules that changed history.
- intermediate · *Elementary Principles of Chemical Processes* · Richard Felder, Ronald Rousseau and Lisa Bullard · latest edition · paid | The standard first textbook on mass and energy balances.
- intermediate · Chemical Engineering courses · MIT OpenCourseWare · ongoing · free | Free university courses on thermodynamics, transport and reactors.
- advanced · *Unit Operations of Chemical Engineering* · Warren McCabe, Julian Smith and Peter Harriott · 7th edition 2005 · paid | A classic on distillation, filtration and other unit operations.
