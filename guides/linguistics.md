# Linguistics

Linguistics is the scientific study of language: its sounds, words, grammar and meaning, how children acquire it, how it varies and changes, and how about 7,000 languages around the world differ and resemble each other.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Language Instinct* · Steven Pinker · 1994 · paid | A lively argument that language is part of human nature.
- beginner · *Babel* · Gaston Dorren · 2018 · paid | A tour of the world's 20 most-spoken languages.
- intermediate · Crash Course Linguistics · Taylor Behnke · 2020 · free | Free short videos on every part of the field.
- intermediate · *Language Files* · Department of Linguistics, Ohio State University · latest edition · paid | A widely used introductory workbook.
- advanced · *An Introduction to Language* · Victoria Fromkin, Robert Rodman and Nina Hyams · latest edition · paid | A standard university textbook.
