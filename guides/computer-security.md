# Computer security

Computer security protects computers, networks and data from attack, theft and damage. It covers passwords and encryption, software flaws, and the people and organisations that attackers target.

## Key works
- beginner · *This Is How They Tell Me the World Ends* · Nicole Perlroth · 2021 · paid | The story of the global market in cyberweapons.
- beginner · *The Cuckoo's Egg* · Clifford Stoll · 1989 · paid | An astronomer tracks a hacker through early networks.
- intermediate · CS50's Introduction to Cybersecurity · Harvard University · 2023 · free | A free course for everyone.
- intermediate · *Security Engineering* · Ross Anderson · 3rd edition 2020 · free-and-paid | The standard text; earlier editions free from the author.
- advanced · *Cryptography Engineering* · Niels Ferguson, Bruce Schneier and Tadayoshi Kohno · 2010 · paid | How cryptography is used correctly.

## Quick check
**Multiple choice.** What is phishing?
- Tricking people into revealing information or clicking harmful links ✓
- A fishing hobby
- A fast internet connection

**Multiple choice.** What does encryption do?
- Scrambles data so only those with the key can read it ✓
- Deletes data
- Speeds up a computer

**Multiple choice.** What is two-factor authentication?
- Proving who you are in two different ways ✓
- Having two passwords for one site
- Two people sharing an account
