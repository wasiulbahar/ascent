# Comparative religion

Comparative religion studies the world's religions side by side: their beliefs, practices, texts, histories and communities, without assuming any one is true or false. It looks for what traditions share and how they differ.

## Key works
- beginner · *The World's Religions* · Huston Smith · 1958 (revised 1991) · paid | A warm introduction to the great traditions.
- beginner · *A History of God* · Karen Armstrong · 1993 · paid | How ideas of God developed in Judaism, Christianity and Islam.
- intermediate · World Religions Through Their Scriptures · Harvard University (edX) · ongoing · free | Free courses on several traditions through their texts (free to audit).
- intermediate · *The World's Religions* · Ninian Smart · 2nd edition 1998 · paid | A scholarly survey organised by dimensions of religion.
- advanced · *The Sacred and the Profane* · Mircea Eliade · 1957 (English 1959) · paid | A classic theory of religious experience.
