# Film studies

Film studies looks at cinema as art, industry and culture: how films are made and how they create meaning through framing, editing, sound and performance, how film history developed around the world, and what critics argue about.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Story of Film* · Mark Cousins · 2004 · paid | A world history of cinema (also a television series).
- beginner · *The Great Movies* · Roger Ebert · 2002 · paid | Short essays on 100 great films by a famous critic.
- intermediate · *Film Art: An Introduction* · David Bordwell and Kristin Thompson · latest edition · paid | The standard textbook on film form and style.
- intermediate · Every Frame a Painting · Tony Zhou and Taylor Ramos · 2014–2016 · free | Free video essays on how films work.
- advanced · *What Is Cinema?* · André Bazin · 1958–62 (English 1967) · paid | Classic essays on film and reality.
