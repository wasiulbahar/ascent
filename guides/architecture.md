# Architecture

Architecture is the art and science of designing buildings and spaces. It balances what the Roman writer Vitruvius called firmness, usefulness and delight: structures that stand up, work for the people in them, and move us.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Architecture of Happiness* · Alain de Botton · 2006 · paid | Why buildings affect how we feel.
- beginner · *Home: A Short History of an Idea* · Witold Rybczynski · 1986 · paid | How the idea of a comfortable home developed.
- intermediate · Architecture (art history lessons) · Smarthistory · ongoing · free | Free essays and videos on buildings across the world.
- intermediate · *Architecture: Form, Space, and Order* · Francis D. K. Ching · latest edition · paid | A classic illustrated guide to design principles.
- advanced · *Modern Architecture: A Critical History* · Kenneth Frampton · 5th edition 2020 · paid | The standard history of modern architecture.
