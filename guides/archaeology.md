# Archaeology

Archaeology studies the human past through what people left behind: tools, buildings, bones, rubbish and landscapes. It reaches back long before writing, and uses methods from excavation to radiocarbon dating and DNA.

## Key works
- beginner · *Archaeology: A Very Short Introduction* · Paul Bahn · 2nd edition 2012 · paid | A short, witty overview.
- beginner · *Prehistory: The Making of the Human Mind* · Colin Renfrew · 2007 · paid | How archaeology explains the human story before writing.
- intermediate · Archaeology's Dirty Little Secrets · Susan Alcock, Brown University · ongoing · free | A free online course (free to audit).
- intermediate · *In the Beginning: An Introduction to Archaeology* · Brian Fagan · latest edition · paid | A long-standing introductory textbook.
- advanced · *Archaeology: Theories, Methods and Practice* · Colin Renfrew and Paul Bahn · latest edition · paid | The standard comprehensive textbook.
