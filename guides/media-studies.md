# Media studies

Media studies examines newspapers, television, film, advertising, the internet and social media: who owns them, how they shape what we know, and how audiences use them.

## Key works
- beginner · *Amusing Ourselves to Death* · Neil Postman · 1985 · paid | How television turned public life into entertainment.
- beginner · *The Attention Merchants* · Tim Wu · 2016 · paid | How advertising has harvested our attention.
- intermediate · *Understanding Media* · Marshall McLuhan · 1964 · paid | “The medium is the message.”
- intermediate · Digital News Report · Reuters Institute, University of Oxford · yearly · free | Free data on how people get news in many countries.
- advanced · *Manufacturing Consent* · Edward S. Herman and Noam Chomsky · 1988 · paid | A model of how media serve powerful interests.
