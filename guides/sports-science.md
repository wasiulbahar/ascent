# Sport and exercise science

Sport and exercise science studies how the body responds to exercise and training, and how to improve performance, health and recovery, drawing on physiology, biomechanics, psychology and nutrition.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Sports Gene* · David Epstein · 2013 · paid | How genes and training combine in athletic success.
- beginner · *Endure* · Alex Hutchinson · 2018 · paid | What limits human endurance: body or mind?
- intermediate · Physical activity fact sheet · World Health Organization · ongoing · free | Free guidance on how much activity people need.
- intermediate · *Exercise Physiology* · William McArdle, Frank Katch and Victor Katch · latest edition · paid | The standard textbook.
- advanced · *Peak* · Anders Ericsson and Robert Pool · 2016 · paid | Deliberate practice and expert performance.
