# Anthropology

Anthropology studies humans in all their variety: cultures and societies around the world, human evolution, language and the past. Its signature method is fieldwork, living with a community to understand life from the inside.

## Key works
- beginner · *Anthro-Vision* · Gillian Tett · 2021 · paid | How anthropology's way of seeing helps in business and life.
- beginner · *Patterns of Culture* · Ruth Benedict · 1934 · paid | A classic that made cultural difference visible to millions.
- intermediate · *Perspectives: An Open Invitation to Cultural Anthropology* · Society for Anthropology in Community Colleges · 2nd edition 2020 · free | A free open textbook.
- intermediate · *Small Places, Large Issues* · Thomas Hylland Eriksen · 4th edition 2015 · paid | A clear introduction to social and cultural anthropology.
- advanced · *The Interpretation of Cultures* · Clifford Geertz · 1973 · paid | Essays that defined interpretive anthropology.
