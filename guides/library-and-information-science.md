# Library and information science

Library and information science studies how information is collected, organised, preserved and found: libraries, archives, catalogues, search, data and information ethics.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Library Book* · Susan Orlean · 2018 · paid | The story of a great library fire, and of what libraries do.
- beginner · *Glut* · Alex Wright · 2007 · paid | A history of how humans have organised information.
- intermediate · *The Discipline of Organizing* · Robert J. Glushko (editor) · 4th edition 2016 · free-and-paid | A textbook on organising information; editions free online.
- intermediate · IFLA resources · International Federation of Library Associations and Institutions · ongoing · free | Free guidelines and reports from libraries worldwide.
- advanced · *Introduction to Information Retrieval* · Christopher Manning, Prabhakar Raghavan and Hinrich Schütze · 2008 · free | The classic on search engines, free online.

## Quick check
**Multiple choice.** What is metadata?
- Data that describes other data, such as a book's author and subject ✓
- A large file
- A broken link

**Multiple choice.** What does a library catalogue do?
- Helps people find items in a collection ✓
- Sells books
- Prints newspapers

**Multiple choice.** What is information retrieval?
- Finding relevant information in large collections ✓
- Throwing information away
- Copying files
