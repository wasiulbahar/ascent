# Psychiatry

Psychiatry is the branch of medicine that studies and treats mental illness, such as depression, anxiety, schizophrenia and bipolar disorder. It combines medicine, psychology and an understanding of people's lives.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Noonday Demon* · Andrew Solomon · 2001 · paid | A deep, humane study of depression.
- beginner · *An Unquiet Mind* · Kay Redfield Jamison · 1995 · paid | A psychiatrist's memoir of her own bipolar disorder.
- intermediate · Mental health fact sheets · World Health Organization · ongoing · free | Free facts on mental disorders worldwide.
- intermediate · *Madness Explained* · Richard Bentall · 2003 · paid | A psychologist questions how psychiatry classifies mental illness.
- advanced · *Kaplan and Sadock's Synopsis of Psychiatry* · Robert Boland and Marcia Verduin (editors) · latest edition · paid | The standard clinical textbook.
