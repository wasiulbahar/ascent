# Cultural studies

Cultural studies looks at everyday culture, from television and music to fashion and food, and how it relates to power, identity, class and politics.

## Key works
- beginner · *Mythologies* · Roland Barthes · 1957 · paid | Short essays reading wrestling, soap powder and wine as myths.
- beginner · *The Uses of Literacy* · Richard Hoggart · 1957 · paid | Working-class culture in England and its changes.
- intermediate · *Encoding and Decoding in the Television Discourse* · Stuart Hall · 1973 · free-and-paid | Audiences read media in different ways; often free online.
- intermediate · *Cultural Theory and Popular Culture* · John Storey · latest edition · paid | A clear textbook of the main theories.
- advanced · *Distinction* · Pierre Bourdieu · 1979 · paid | How taste marks social class.
