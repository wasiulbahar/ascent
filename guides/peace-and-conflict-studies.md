# Peace and conflict studies

Peace and conflict studies examines why wars and violent conflicts happen, how they end, and how lasting peace can be built.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Better Angels of Our Nature* · Steven Pinker · 2011 · paid | Argues violence has declined over history; strongly debated.
- beginner · *Why We Fight* · Christopher Blattman · 2022 · paid | Five reasons wars happen, and how to prevent them.
- intermediate · Uppsala Conflict Data Program · Uppsala University · ongoing · free | Free data on armed conflicts worldwide.
- intermediate · *Peace by Peaceful Means* · Johan Galtung · 1996 · paid | Galtung's theory of positive and negative peace.
- advanced · *Making War and Building Peace* · Michael Doyle and Nicholas Sambanis · 2006 · paid | A study of UN peace operations.

## Quick check
**Multiple choice.** What is “negative peace”, in Galtung's terms?
- The absence of violence ✓
- A bad peace treaty
- A war

**Multiple choice.** What is “positive peace”?
- Peace with justice and cooperation, not just no war ✓
- Peace in one city
- A happy song

**Multiple choice.** What does the Uppsala Conflict Data Program provide?
- Data on armed conflicts ✓
- Weather data
- Football data
