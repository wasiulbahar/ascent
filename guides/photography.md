# Photography

Photography studies how pictures are made with light and cameras, and how photographs work as art, evidence, journalism and memory. It began in the 1820s–1830s and now fills every phone.

## Key works
- beginner · *On Photography* · Susan Sontag · 1977 · paid | Classic essays on what photographs do to us.
- beginner · *The Photographer's Eye* · John Szarkowski · 1966 · paid | A museum curator on how photographs see.
- intermediate · *Camera Lucida* · Roland Barthes · 1980 · paid | A short, personal book on looking at photographs.
- intermediate · Photography collections · The Museum of Modern Art and other museums · ongoing · free | Free online collections of great photographs.
- advanced · *A World History of Photography* · Naomi Rosenblum · 4th edition 2007 · paid | A standard global history.
