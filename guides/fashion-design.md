# Fashion design

Fashion design creates clothing and accessories, combining art, craft, materials, business and culture. Fashion studies also asks what clothes say about identity and society, and about the industry's effects on workers and the planet.

## Key works
- beginner · *Fashionopolis* · Dana Thomas · 2019 · paid | How fast fashion harms people and the planet, and the alternatives.
- beginner · *The Fashion System* · Roland Barthes · 1967 · paid | How the language of fashion magazines creates meaning.
- intermediate · *The Fundamentals of Fashion Design* · Richard Sorger and Jenny Udale · 3rd edition 2017 · paid | An introduction to the design process.
- intermediate · Fashion collections · The Metropolitan Museum of Art (Costume Institute) and the V&A · ongoing · free | Free online collections of historic dress.
- advanced · *Adorned in Dreams* · Elizabeth Wilson · 1985 (revised 2003) · paid | A classic cultural history of fashion.
