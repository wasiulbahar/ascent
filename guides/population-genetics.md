# Population genetics

Population genetics studies how the frequencies of gene variants change in populations over time, through natural selection, genetic drift, mutation and migration. It turned Darwin's idea of evolution into mathematics in the early twentieth century, and today it reads ancient DNA to trace human history.

## Key works
- beginner · *Why Evolution Is True* · Jerry Coyne · 2009 · paid | The evidence for evolution, clearly explained.
- beginner · *Who We Are and How We Got Here* · David Reich · 2018 · paid | What ancient DNA reveals about human migrations.
- intermediate · *Population Genetics: A Concise Guide* · John H. Gillespie · 2nd edition 2004 · paid | A short, friendly introduction to the core models.
- intermediate · *Population and Quantitative Genetics* (lecture notes) · Graham Coop · ongoing · free | Free online notes from a university course.
- advanced · *Principles of Population Genetics* · Daniel Hartl and Andrew Clark · latest edition · paid | The standard advanced textbook.
