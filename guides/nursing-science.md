# Nursing

Nursing is the care of people who are ill, recovering, giving birth or growing old, and the science behind that care. Nurses are the largest group of health workers in the world, and nursing research studies how care can be safer and kinder.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Language of Kindness* · Christie Watson · 2018 · paid | A nurse's account of twenty years of care.
- beginner · *Notes on Nursing* · Florence Nightingale · 1859 · free | The founding text of modern nursing, out of copyright.
- intermediate · *State of the World's Nursing 2020* · World Health Organization · 2020 · free | A free report on nurses around the world.
- intermediate · *Fundamentals of Nursing* · OpenStax · 2024 · free | A free open textbook.
- advanced · *Nursing Research* · Denise Polit and Cheryl Tatano Beck · latest edition · paid | The standard text on research methods in nursing.
