# Marine biology

The ocean covers most of Earth's surface and holds much of its life, from microscopic plankton to the blue whale. Marine biology studies sea life and its habitats, from coral reefs to the deep sea, and how fishing, pollution and warming waters are changing them.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Sea Around Us* · Rachel Carson · 1951 · paid | A classic portrait of the ocean by the author of *Silent Spring*.
- beginner · *The Ocean of Life* · Callum Roberts · 2012 · paid | How human activity is changing the sea.
- intermediate · Ocean education resources · NOAA (US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) · ongoing · free | Free lessons and data on ocean life and habitats.
- intermediate · *Marine Biology* · Peter Castro and Michael Huber · latest edition · paid | An introductory university textbook.
- advanced · *Marine Biology: Function, Biodiversity, Ecology* · Jeffrey Levinton · latest edition · paid | An advanced text on how marine life works.
