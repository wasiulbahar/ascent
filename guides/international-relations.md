# International relations

International relations studies how states and other actors interact: war and peace, trade, diplomacy, international law and organisations such as the UN.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Twenty Years' Crisis* · E. H. Carr · 1939 · paid | A classic realist critique of idealism between the world wars.
- beginner · *World Order* · Henry Kissinger · 2014 · paid | How different civilisations have imagined world order.
- intermediate · *Theory of International Politics* · Kenneth Waltz · 1979 · paid | The founding text of neorealism.
- intermediate · *International Relations Theory* · E-International Relations (Stephen McGlinchey and others) · 2017 · free | A free textbook on the main theories.
- advanced · *Social Theory of International Politics* · Alexander Wendt · 1999 · paid | Constructivism: “anarchy is what states make of it”.
