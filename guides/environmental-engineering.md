# Environmental engineering

Environmental engineering protects people and the environment: clean water supply, sanitation, waste treatment, air-pollution control and cleaning up contaminated land.

## Key works
- beginner · *The Big Necessity* · Rose George · 2008 · paid | The world's sanitation problem, and why it matters.
- beginner · *Water 4.0* · David Sedlak · 2014 · paid | The past and future of urban water systems.
- intermediate · WHO/UNICEF Joint Monitoring Programme · World Health Organization and UNICEF · ongoing · free | Free data on water and sanitation worldwide.
- intermediate · *Introduction to Environmental Engineering* · Mackenzie Davis and David Cornwell · latest edition · paid | A standard textbook.
- advanced · *Wastewater Engineering* · Metcalf & Eddy (AECOM) · latest edition · paid | The standard reference on treating wastewater.
